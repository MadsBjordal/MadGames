## 2. Hardcore vs mainstream

| | Hardcore | Mainstream mobile |
|---|---|---|
| Wants | interlocking systems, real choices/builds, offline progress, endings/milestones, active dev | short sessions, offline earnings, tidy upgrades, collecting |
| Monetisation | pays once $5–20; hates ad/P2W-balanced games | accepts opt-in rewarded; hates forced ads |
| Length | split: 80–100 h to years vs 2–10 h Nodebuster-likes | endless with dailies/events |
| Platform | browser, Steam, Discord | phone, portrait |

Scale: Idle Miner Tycoon 150M downloads (Sep 2022, Kolibri press). The Tower – Idle Tower Defense ~$65M+ lifetime, 4 staff (DataEye estimate), ~5.9M Android installs (AppGoblin estimate). Idle Restaurant Tycoon ~5.5M installs (AppGoblin estimate).

## 3. Frustrations

1. Forced ads / ad-gated progress. Top reply (61 pts): "People don't mind ads, they mind when the balance of the game was formed with ads and/or P2W" (leaderboards needing 10+ ads/hour). Landscape-only PC ports put players off (44 pts).
2. Paid ad removal not fully working (review aggregator, anecdotal).
3. Boring prestige ("negligible benefits"); too frequent or too punishing.
4. Number goes up without real progress (enemies scale with you).
5. Too short, little automation, no offline progress.
6. Stagnation of long games; endless games with no reason to play.
7. Abandoned games and copycats.
8. Lost saves and time gates: little direct 2025–26 evidence found (*guess:* still pain points).
9. Players review against the label (negative after 12 h, then play 80 more).

## 4. Ads

- Opt-in rewarded broadly tolerated if "strictly limited and not required for progression". Tapjoy 2019: 76% prefer opt-in; Gameloft Jul 2025: 64% find rewarded acceptable (vendor surveys).
- One-time ad removal seen as fair; subscriptions disliked. Pressure to pay is the dealbreaker.
- Steam premium idles cheap: $2–5 on sale.

## 5. What retains for months/years

Interlocking new systems with different rules; continuous updates + Discord; offline progress and short check-ins (GameAnalytics: idle DAU/MAU 18% vs 10.5% hyper-casual); dailies/events/collection; prestige that changes play; portrait UI; surprise/discovery.

## 7. Themes

Overdone (*inference*): mining, generic tycoon/restaurant, farming, Nodebuster clones. Asked-for mechanics: meaningful choices/builds, automation, unusual mechanics without prestige, RTS/army incrementals.
