## Review themes (Play "most relevant", Steam top English)

- AdVenture Capitalist: intrusive ads (one opens with a menu), ~$15/mo ad-free plan seen as money grab, ads crash/reset and reward lost. + simple, events.
- AdVenture Communist: rework removed purchases without refund; 3+ min ads with scam content; stalls ~rank 16 without paying. + long-term time-killer.
- AdVenture Ages: tiny close buttons, ads opening the store; $10/mo premium still shows ads; "same game re-skinned"; P2W events.
- Idle Miner Tycoon: pop-up flood even for no-ads buyers; sync bugs, weak support; "plays itself", little agency. + relaxing.
- Idle Factory Tycoon: reworks that locked production; 24 h unlock timers.
- Egg, Inc.: + fully optional ads, never begs; daily calendar that doesn't punish missed days; humour; co-op.
- Idle Planet Miner: tournament P2W; grindy. + no-ads purchase worth it; depth.
- Cell to Singularity: ad failures, crashes, lost progress after updates. + educational, calm, content, worthwhile rewarded ads, not pushy.
- Antimatter Dimensions: long walls. + best in genre, layered resets, discreet shop, finishable (~194 days / ~640 h).
- NGU Idle: + depth, honest, no aggressive monetization.
- Trimps: + depth, generous offline, optional Bones, attentive dev.
- Leaf Blower Revolution: + depth/lore, new mechanics keep appearing, no ads. − wants more offline.
- Realm Grinder: + factions make runs differ, secrets. − no sound, slow gaps, flat late game.
- Kittens Game: + no ads/pressure, deep, QoL updates.
- Synergism: − confusing UI, premium-currency menus can't be hidden, too many stacked modifiers. + NGU-level depth.
- Unnamed Space Idle: − too many terms and tabs too early on mobile, small buttons, grindy. + battle view, fair pacing.
- Farmer Against Potatoes: + content, no ads in practice, offline, earnable premium. − crude art, cluttered phone UI.
- Idle Wizard: + not P2W, deep, active dev. − harsh resets, ~10 currencies within 2 h.
- Your Chronicle: + story-driven, endings, everything earnable. − QoL behind paywall, wants dark mode.
- Melvor Idle: − won't run when servers are down, free/full paywall, Play 3.7. + "RuneScape without playing it".
- Idle Research: − forced landscape, mixed text sizes, little explanation.
- Swarm Simulator: − need ~$10 no-ads, clunky UI, slow first ascension (~4 days), tracking-software report. + graphics.
- Idle Slayer: − dev slowed game/removed idle options, active-only minigames, failed rewarded ads. + art, music.
- Tap Titans 2: + polished, ads by choice, events. − inventory bugs.
- Idle Lumber Empire: − crashes on return from ads, reward lost; 10-minute ads.
- Eatventure: + no forced ads, progress without paying. − repetitive later.
- Idle Outpost: − stingy events, 120 s ads without reward, chat without block.
- Revolution Idle: + strategy keeps opening, earnable premium. − mid-game crawl needs Discord guide, limited offline, monetization changes upset payers.
- Exponential Idle: + cheap permanent ad bonus, maths theme, nothing behind ads.
- Shop Titans: − unworkable without paying, sub, refund disputes. + art.
- Idle Bank Tycoon: − "wrapper around ads/spending".
- Make It Rain: − ads "every five seconds".

Patterns: 1–2 star reviews come from forced/long ads, ads that fail or crash, subscriptions that still show ads, pop-up floods, P2W events. High scores come from fully optional ads, earnable premium currency, real offline progress, systems that keep opening, developers who talk to players.

## Reported revenue and deals

- Kolibri: Ubisoft 75% stake completed 31 Jan 2020 (price undisclosed); Wikipedia: €53.5M revenue (FT 2021 list), €160M valuation; IMT + IFT ~$1.6M/month (2020 report); analyst estimate ~$30M for IMT in 2019.
- Kongregate: MTG bought it for $55M (2017); merged into Monumental Feb 2024 (MTG keeps 30%). No evidence Kongregate acquired Egg, Inc.
- Egg, Inc.: AppGoblin estimate > $1M/month iOS IAP.
- Hyper Hippo: AdVenture games 80M+ installs total; AdCap 55M+ mobile downloads (pocketgamer.biz).
- Lessmore (Eatventure) → Miniclip, closed late 2024, announced Mar 2025; AppMagic estimate ~$29.4M IAP, 69.3M downloads.
